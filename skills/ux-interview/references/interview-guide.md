# Interview Guide — Extended Question Bank

Use these prompts when a phase needs more depth or the conversation stalls.

## Phase 1: Context & Background

- What would you say is the primary job this system does for you?
- Who else uses this system alongside you? Do you collaborate through it?
- Is this system part of a larger workflow — do you use other tools before or after?
- How did you end up using this system? Was it your choice or assigned?

## Phase 2: Tasks & Workflows

### Getting step-by-step detail
- What's the very first thing you do when you open it?
- After that, what happens next?
- How do you know when that step is done?
- Where does the information you need come from? Do you have to look it up elsewhere?
- What does the output look like? Where does it go?

### Uncovering variation
- Is there a version of this task that's more complicated or unusual? What does that look like?
- Are there times when the normal flow breaks down? What do you do then?
- Do different days or situations change how you approach this?

### Discovering workarounds
- Is there anything you do outside this system to make it work better — like keeping a spreadsheet or using a separate tool?
- Have you ever automated part of this yourself?

## Phase 3: Pain Points

### Friction
- If you had to rate your frustration with this system on a scale of 1–10, what would you say?
- What's the thing you complain about most to colleagues?
- Is there a step where you always have to be extra careful not to make a mistake?

### Errors & recovery
- What happens when something goes wrong? How do you find out?
- How long does it typically take to recover from a mistake?

### Time & effort
- Which part of this workflow takes the most time?
- Are there tasks that feel like they shouldn't require this much effort?

## Phase 4: Positives

- Is there a feature you think is genuinely clever or well-designed?
- What part of this system do you show new people when onboarding them?
- Is there a moment in the workflow that just feels smooth and easy?

## Phase 5: Wishes & Ideas

- If you had unlimited budget and engineering time, what would you build first?
- Is there a feature you've requested before that hasn't been built yet?
- Have you ever switched from another system to this one? What did you leave behind that you miss?
- What would make you say "this system is finally perfect"?

## Phase 6: Wrap-up

- Is there a question I didn't ask that you were expecting?
- If you could leave one note for the team building the next version, what would it say?
- Is there anyone else who uses this system differently than you do, who might be worth talking to?

## Active Listening Phrases

Use these to reflect and verify understanding:

- "So just to make sure I've got this — [paraphrase]. Did I capture that right?"
- "It sounds like the biggest friction point there is [X]. Is that fair?"
- "I'm hearing two things: [A] and [B]. Are both equally important, or does one outweigh the other?"
- "You mentioned [X] earlier — is that connected to what you're describing now?"
- "That's interesting. Can you tell me more about that?"
- "Can you give me a concrete example of a time that happened?"
